How to calculate a percentage

To find a percentage of a number, divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. For example, 15% of 240 is (15 ÷ 100) × 240 = 36.

To determine what percentage 36 is of 240, divide 36 by 240 and multiply by 100: 15%.

Why percent change can be misleading

A 20% drop followed by a 20% increase does not return to the starting point. $100 falls to $80, then rises to $96. The base changes between steps.

Our percentage-change and reverse-percentage tools help you distinguish the original base from the new value.

Common percentage questions

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

An interest rate rising from 4% to 5% rises by one percentage point, but the relative percentage increase is 25%.

Can a percentage exceed 100%?

Yes. A value of 300 compared with a base of 200 is 150% of that base. Percentages can also be negative when describing a change.

Why do my results differ by a cent?

Rounding at intermediate steps can change final cents. These tools calculate with JavaScript numbers and round displayed results, so verify monetary totals against your actual invoice or checkout.
