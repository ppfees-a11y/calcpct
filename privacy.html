CalcPct provides free browser-based percentage calculators. No account is required to use the calculators.

Analytics

Third-Party Services

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies. CalcPct does not control how those independent providers process information.

Children's Privacy

CalcPct is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website, providers, or applicable practices change.
